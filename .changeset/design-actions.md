The assistant can now work on a project's requirements and decisions. It reads them, proposes a requirement, proposes a decision with the options weighed, and proposes to replace a decision that no longer holds: the new one is recorded and the old one stays, marked as superseded by it. Nothing is written before the owner accepts the card, which shows every option with what speaks for and against it. Behind the `design-records` flag.
