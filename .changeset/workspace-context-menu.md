A right-click in an architecture's workspace opens a menu on a view, an element, a relation, a member or a step of a sequence: its editing actions, then "Add to the assistant's context". A view or a part of one becomes a chip next to the prompt, and a message about items of one architecture goes straight to its diagram assistant with those views and the parts pointed at.
