The assistant manages agent conventions at every level: it can change their name, notes, tools and project, stop holding a ready-made rule, add or remove the skills they hold, merge several of them (asking which source to keep for each conflict) and suggest roles. It also reads their tools, notes and skills, and `find_skills` says which skills they can hold.
