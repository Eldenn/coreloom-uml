Behind the `assistant-actions` flag, the assistant decides by itself what a message needs: an answer, work on an architecture's diagrams, rules read from the web, or actions on your work. "Auto" becomes the default subject of a message; the other choices stay to force one. The assistant can propose creating or renaming a project, creating, renaming or duplicating an architecture, and adding, editing, removing or holding rules of agent conventions. Each proposal is a card to accept or reject: nothing changes before you accept it.
