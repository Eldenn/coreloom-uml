« Design from a brief » on a project's Design tab: the owner says what they need to build, and the assistant leads a new conversation from it, one step at a time, to requirements, then to one decision with its options weighed, then to an architecture and its views. It asks first when the brief leaves out what the design depends on, and every step is a card the owner accepts. Behind the `design-records`, `assistant-actions` and `diagram-assistant` flags.
