A workspace assistant in the architecture editor, behind the `diagram-assistant` flag: conversations kept per architecture, answered by the user's own model, that read the architecture's views and propose view changes shown as diffs to accept, reject or undo, with a context gauge and a way to continue a long conversation in a new one from its summary.
